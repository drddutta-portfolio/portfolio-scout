## 4. Canonical identity

One security per authoritative ISIN. An instrument listed on both NSE and BSE
with the same ISIN becomes **one** row: `exchange`/`primary_symbol` hold the
NSE listing when present (BSE otherwise), and both listings are preserved as
`EXCHANGE_SYMBOL` aliases (BSE also contributes its numeric scrip code). This
satisfies the M04 partial unique on ISIN and on (exchange, primary_symbol).

Row IDs are UUIDv5 derived from a fixed namespace and the canonical key, so
regenerating the artifact reproduces identical IDs — safe for idempotent
re-application and for stable historical references.

### Exclusions (7,421 source records)
- 7,417 out-of-scope BSE segments (debentures/bonds, commercial paper,
  preference shares, blank-segment rows) — Phase 1 covers listed equity and
  exchange-listed funds/trusts only.
- 3 rights-entitlement rows (temporary instruments).
- 1 malformed ISIN.

### SME decision
NSE Emerge SME equities **are included** (569 securities). They are real
holdings and are not excluded for being SME. Only rights-entitlement series
rows were dropped, on the basis that they are temporary instruments, not SME
status.

### REIT / InvIT
27 BSE `IF`-group trust rows resolve to 9 REITs and 18 InvITs. Classification
uses the ISIN instrument-type positions (25 = REIT, 23 = InvIT) and every one
was cross-read against the actual trust names (Embassy, Mindspace, Brookfield,
Nexus, Bagmane for REIT; IRB, IndiGrid, POWERGRID, NHIT, Cube Highways and
peers for InvIT). Names and codes agree with the ISIN evidence in all 27 cases.

### Conflicts (126 — none silently resolved)
- **68 fund units not on any exchange ETF list.** INF-space ISINs appearing
  only in BSE's equity segment. Some are genuine BSE-only ETFs, some are
  segregated mutual-fund portfolios. The sources cannot separate them, so they
  are seeded as `UNKNOWN` rather than mislabelled `ETF`.
- **58 ambiguous aliases.** A normalized alias value claimed by two different
  securities in the same context. Both sides are withheld; the alias is not
  seeded and matching stays deterministic.

### Notes (2,689 — informational)
NSE and BSE spell the same company differently ("Ltd" vs "Limited"). Both
spellings are kept as `COMPANY_NAME` aliases, so either import wording matches.
